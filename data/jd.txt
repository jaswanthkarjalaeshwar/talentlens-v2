Senior Product Manager — AI/ML Platform

About the Role
We are looking for a Senior Product Manager to lead our AI/ML Platform product, working at the intersection of infrastructure, data science, and internal developer tooling. You will own the roadmap for the platform that powers all ML-driven features across the company.

Responsibilities
- Define and execute the product vision and multi-quarter roadmap for the AI/ML Platform
- Partner closely with ML engineers, data scientists, and platform engineers to ship infrastructure that improves model development velocity
- Translate ambiguous business problems into crisp product requirements and acceptance criteria
- Lead cross-functional alignment across Engineering, Data Science, Design, and GTM teams
- Drive prioritization decisions using quantitative data, customer interviews, and business impact modeling
- Define and track success metrics for platform adoption and developer productivity
- Communicate product strategy and progress to senior leadership (VP/C-suite)
- Mentor junior PMs on the team

Required Skills
- 5+ years of product management experience, with at least 2 years managing technical or infrastructure products
- Strong understanding of the ML lifecycle: data collection, feature engineering, model training, deployment, and monitoring
- Experience defining APIs, SDKs, or developer-facing platform products
- Proficiency with data analysis and metrics (SQL, A/B testing, product analytics tools)
- Excellent written and verbal communication; able to write crisp PRDs and present to executives
- Track record of shipping complex cross-functional projects on time

Preferred Skills
- Previous experience at a growth-stage or large tech company in an ML/AI product role
- Familiarity with cloud ML platforms (AWS SageMaker, GCP Vertex AI, or Azure ML)
- Experience with feature stores, experiment tracking (MLflow, W&B), or model serving frameworks
- MBA or graduate degree in a quantitative field

Culture & Values
- We move fast and value bias to action over perfect planning
- We are deeply data-driven — intuition is a starting point, not a conclusion
- We believe in strong ownership: PMs here own outcomes, not just outputs
- Collaborative, low-ego environment where the best idea wins regardless of seniority
- Remote-first with quarterly in-person off-sites
